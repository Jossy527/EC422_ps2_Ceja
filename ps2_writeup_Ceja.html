It includes the U.S. national unemployment rate and unemployment rates of Oregon, California, Texas, and New York. The series it contains runs from January 1976 through the most recent observation available.

##Visualization

Here, I visualize the patterns in unemployment using the wrangled datasets. The plots below will show trends over time, regional differences, and patterns that exclude the COVID period.

1. Line Plot of Unemployment Over Time: this shows the unemployment rates for each region

2. Faceted Line Plot by Region: this shows each region’s unemployment rate, viewed separately to compare easier.

3. Bar Chart of Mean Unemployment Rate: this shows the average unemployment rates by region, going from lowest to highest.

4. Unemployment Rate (Excluding 2020): this plot was chosen to show how unemployment behaved over the years, excluding 2020 to make the trends easier to observe without the extreme pandemic spike.
